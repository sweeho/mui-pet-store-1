# Customer accounts

## Why
Recover the legacy pet store's customer, sign-on, account, contact and profile behaviour as a verifiable specification for the rebuild.

## What changes
- Adds 23 requirements for sign-on, session handling, account creation, and the customer, account, contact, credit card and profile data model.

## Impact
- Capability: customer-accounts. Related capabilities (checkout, payment, catalog) are specified separately.
